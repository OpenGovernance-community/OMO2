# Technology illustrations

Square illustrations for `/popup/technologies.php`. All images are local; opening the page makes no third-party image requests.

- Brand symbols from Simple Icons 15.0.0 keep their original shapes and brand colors. See `SIMPLE-ICONS-LICENSE.md` and the per-icon source and license metadata in `sources.json`.
- Summernote uses the project organization avatar; Etherpad and Playwright use their own project logos. Upstream URLs are recorded in `sources.json`. The Etherpad thumbnail is static, with increased opacity for legibility. These logos remain the property of their respective projects.
- Other illustrations are original OMO2 category drawings and monograms, not official project logos.
- The square backgrounds and framing are local presentation assets. When adding a technology, add its illustration and record its origin in `sources.json`.
